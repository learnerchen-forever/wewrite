**Insert Image from System** picks an image from the device's own photo library and copies it into the vault before embedding it — the one route that reaches a phone's camera roll from inside Obsidian, and the reason browsing the filesystem from the app found nothing on Huawei HarmonyOS. Formats Obsidian cannot display are converted to PNG on the way in; a file the browser itself cannot decode (some HEIC variants) now says so instead of quietly embedding a broken image.
