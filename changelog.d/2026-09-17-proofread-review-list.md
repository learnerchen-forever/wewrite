The proofreading review is now a list you can scan before deciding anything: every correction with its type colour-coded by severity (spelling and punctuation red, grammar orange, wording blue), click to jump, only the changed characters marked in the original, and a suggestion you can rewrite before accepting. Corrections can be accepted or ignored a type at a time, and the review ends with a count of what was applied.
