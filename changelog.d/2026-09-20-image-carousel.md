The image window is a carousel now: each picture fills the frame on its own and a swipe turns to the next one, instead of a strip of pictures you had to drag sideways to see. The turning is done with `scroll-snap`, so it survives publishing — WeChat runs no scripts in an article — and a mixed run of portrait and landscape pictures centres the current one in the frame. The switch and its parameter are unchanged.
